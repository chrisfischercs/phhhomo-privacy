Phhhomo Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026. Phhhomo is a small app for sharing short looping photos with friends. This page explains what it collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, password (stored hashed by our login provider), and the username you pick.
  • What you post: the loops you share, captions, comments, likes, follows, your bio and profile photo.
  • Location, only if you add it: if you tag a place on a post, the place name and its coordinates are saved with that post. Your phone's location is used to find nearby places while you pick one, and is not stored otherwise.
  • Songs, only if you add them: the song title, artist and an id from Apple, Deezer or Audius. Search words you type in the song picker are sent to that service.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a device token so we can send you push notifications.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report or block someone, we keep that record.

Phhhomo has no ads, no analytics and no tracking of you across other apps or sites.

Who can see it

Posts, comments, profile photo, bio, follower counts and any place or song you add are visible to other Phhhomo users. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and file host. Push notifications are delivered through Apple. We do not sell your data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the You tab and tap "Delete account". This permanently removes your login, profile, posts, comments, likes, follows and files. You can also delete individual posts from the menu on each post.

Children

Phhhomo is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
